In brief. Left breast invasive ductal carcinoma diagnosed 4/2015 (2.1 cm, 1 of 3 nodes, ER/PR-positive, HER2-negative), treated with lumpectomy, AC → paclitaxel, radiation and endocrine therapy. Bone recurrence 2/2021 (T10 spine and right ilium). Since then four lines of therapy: letrozole + palbociclib; fulvestrant + alpelisib (PIK3CA mutation); capecitabine; and sacituzumab govitecan (current, partial response in the liver on CT 1/12/2026). One admission, for dehydration on capecitabine (2/2025).

1. Disease at a glance

  • Diagnosis, 4/2015: screening mammogram 4/14/2015; lumpectomy and sentinel node biopsy 5/6/2015: 2.1 cm IDC, 1 of 3 nodes.
  • Metastatic recurrence, 2/2021: PET 2/18/2021: T10 and right iliac bone lesions; biopsy of T10 2/24/2021: ER 90%, PR 0%, HER2 0.
  • Tumour DNA, 5/2023: liquid biopsy: PIK3CA H1047R; ESR1 not detected.
  • Now (9/2026): on sacituzumab govitecan at 7.5 mg/kg; liver disease responding; CA 27-29 47 U/mL (9/15/2026).
